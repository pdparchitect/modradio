# Using ModRadio

ModRadio discovers tracker music through BassoonTracker and The Mod Archive and plays it locally. Downloaded tracks stay in memory.

Open ModRadio from Applications. It runs in the menu bar without a Dock icon. Choose **Play Random MOD or XM** to start listening.

The radio alternates between BassoonTracker’s random MOD and XM catalogues. **Play Another Track** skips to the next song. Pause and resume from the menu or your Mac’s media controls. **Stop Radio** ends playback. The volume slider remembers your preference.

Most tracks show elapsed and estimated total time and can be seeked with the progress slider. Rare legacy MOD files handled by the native fallback show elapsed time only. Tracks advance after their first complete traversal instead of looping indefinitely.

While one track plays, the next is downloaded, validated, and held in memory. Natural completion and manual skips use this prepared track when available. A network connection is required; ModRadio does not save music for offline playback.

macOS Now Playing displays the current title, artist when available, duration, and playback state. Play/pause pauses and resumes; next skips; previous returns to the start; supported skip controls seek by 15 seconds.

**Open in BassoonTracker** and **View Module Page** open the current song’s external pages in your browser.
